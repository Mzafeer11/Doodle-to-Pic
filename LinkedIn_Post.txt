🎨 From Simple Doodles to Realistic Art: Pix2Pix Conditional GANs

I recently completed a project focused on "Doodle-to-Real" translation using the Pix2Pix framework. The goal was to take sparse user sketches and synthesize realistic, textured images.

Technical Strategy:
🛠️ U-Net Generator: Integrated skip connections between encoder and decoder blocks to preserve low-level spatial features from the original doodle.
🛠️ PatchGAN Discriminator: Used a 70x70 PatchGAN architecture to penalize structure at the scale of local patches rather than the entire image, ensuring crisp high-frequency detail.
🛠️ Performance Optimization: Scaled the pipeline using PyTorch's Mixed Precision (AMP) to achieve a 40% memory reduction during training.

Check out the modular codebase and results here: https://github.com/Mzafeer11/Doodle-to-Pic
