🎨 Transforming Doodles to Reality with Pix2Pix!

I'm excited to share my implementation of a Pixar-style Pix2Pix model. It takes simple user-drawn doodles and translates them into realistic, textured images using Conditional GANs.

Highlights:
- U-Net Generator with skip connections to maintain spatial precision.
- Conditional Discriminator for accurate image attribution.

Repo: https://github.com/Mzafeer11/Doodle-to-Pic
